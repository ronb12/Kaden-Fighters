Kaden Fighters Privacy Policy

Kaden Fighters: Rise of Reigen is a local fighting game. This policy explains the small amount of gameplay data used by the scoreboard.

Data The Game Stores

The game may save your chosen scoreboard name, match results, high score, fighter choice, combo count, and local leaderboard preferences on your device.

Global Scores

If you submit a score to the global leaderboard, the game sends your scoreboard name and score to the Kaden Fighters server so the ranking list can be shown to players.

Tracking And Ads

The game does not use advertising, third-party ad tracking, location, camera, microphone, contacts, or photo library access.

Deletion

You can reset local data by clearing website/app data on your device. For global scoreboard removal, contact the developer with the scoreboard name you want removed.
